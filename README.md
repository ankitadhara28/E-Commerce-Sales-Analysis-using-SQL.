# 📊 E-Commerce Sales Analysis using SQL

## 🚀 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing an e-commerce dataset to extract meaningful business insights using SQL. The analysis covers sales performance, product trends, regional distribution, and profitability.

The project also includes data visualization using Python (Matplotlib) to better understand key patterns and insights.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze total sales and revenue performance
- Identify top-performing products
- Evaluate regional sales distribution
- Determine most profitable product categories
- Detect loss-making products
- Visualize insights using charts

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
- SQL (SQLite)
- Python
- Google Colab
- Pandas
- Matplotlib

---

## 📂 Dataset
- Superstore Sales Dataset (CSV)
- Contains information on:
  - Orders
  - Customers
  - Products
  - Sales & Profit
  - Regions and Categories

---

## 🔍 Key Analysis Performed

### 1. Total Sales
Calculated overall revenue generated from all orders.

### 2. Top Products by Sales
Identified products contributing the highest revenue.

### 3. Sales by Region
Analyzed which region generates the most sales.

### 4. Profit by Category
Evaluated profitability across product categories.

### 5. Loss-Making Products
Detected products that are causing financial losses.

---

## 📊 Visualizations
- Sales by Region (Bar Chart)
- Profit by Category (Bar Chart)
- Top 5 Products by Sales (Horizontal Bar Chart)
- Top 10 Loss-Making Products (Horizontal Bar Chart)

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- Total sales reached approximately **2.29 million**, indicating strong business performance.
- The **West region** generated the highest revenue among all regions.
- **Technology** is the most profitable category, followed by Office Supplies.
- Premium products contribute significantly to total revenue.
- Several products are incurring losses, indicating a need for pricing or strategy optimization.

---

## 🧠 Conclusion
The analysis highlights strong revenue performance but also reveals areas for improvement, particularly in managing loss-making products and optimizing category-level profitability.

This project demonstrates how SQL and data visualization can be used together to derive actionable business insights.
